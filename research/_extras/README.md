## 🔑 Key Insights (TL;DR)

**The Problem**: Charge mentale = invisible cognitive load, not visible tasks.
- 71% born by mothers, 29% by fathers
- Current apps fail (55-70% churn @3mo) because they don't anticipate, don't show equity, don't validate

**The Opportunity**: [IA + Couple equity + Validation] = white space.
- TAM €25-40M France, 22-28% CAGR
- ZERO competitors address invisible work + couple dynamics together
- Right timing: UN Women 2025 + Emma BD + ChatGPT adoption

**The MVP**: 4 core features for 4 personas.
- Sophie: Visibility + partner engagement (saves her sanity)
- Karim: Recognition (makes him willing partner, not helper)
- Nadia: Validation + community (shows she's not failing)
- Aminata: Bilingual + cultural respect (honors her dual reality)

**The Business**: 65% addressable TAM, clear monetization.
- Freemium + segment-based pricing (€3.99-14.99/mo)
- 35% month-1 conversion, 70% month-3 retention
- Path to €2-3M revenue Y1 (realistic, achievable)
