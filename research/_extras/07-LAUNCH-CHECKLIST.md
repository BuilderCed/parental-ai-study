## Go/No-Go Decision Points

### After Phase 1 (Week 10)
- ✅ **GO if** : 60%+ users resonate, RESPIRE wins naming test, safe messaging > 85%
- 🔄 **PIVOT if** : <60% resonate (revisit positioning), ANTICIPA wins (rename), messaging concerns
- ❌ **NO-GO if** : <40% resonate, privacy concerns > 50%, can't differentiate from Cozi

### After Phase 2 (Week 16)
- ✅ **GO if** : Brand identity tested, messaging positive, testimonials collected
- 🔄 **PIVOT if** : Logo/colors underperform, copy needs refinement
- ❌ **NO-GO if** : Brand doesn't differentiate, messaging triggers guilt

### After Phase 3 (Week 24)
- ✅ **GO if** : Community engaged, experts supportive, influencers on board
- 🔄 **PIVOT if** : Community feedback reveals product issues, repositioning needed
- ❌ **NO-GO if** : Can't build community, expert endorsements rejected, product unready

### Before Phase 5 (Week 28)
- ✅ **GO if** : All validation complete, team confident, product stable
- ❌ **NO-GO if** : Critical bugs remain, GDPR issues, market shifts
