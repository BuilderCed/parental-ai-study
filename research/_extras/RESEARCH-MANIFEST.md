## Limitations Acknowledged

1. **CSP+ bias** — Easier to find articulate voices in written forums (may under-represent all-publics pain)
2. **France-centric** — Research focused on FR/FR-speaking. EU applicability high, Africa/Asia = different
3. **Snapshot in time** — Parenting trends shift. Data = Feb 2026
4. **Online bias** — May miss offline, non-digital-native parents (rare in target segment)
5. **Selection bias** — People complaining in forums ≠ representative (but pain intensity likely understated)

**Mitigations** :
- Cross-reference with academic studies (reduce bias)
- Segment secondary analysis for low-CSP (in roadmap)
- Plan validation interviews (next phase)
