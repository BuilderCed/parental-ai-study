3. MEDIUM: CNIL consultation delays (40-60% probability)
   → 2-3 month launch delay
   Mitigation: Commission DPIA immediately, expect consultation

4. MEDIUM: Domestic abuse misuse (location surveillance)
   → Lawsuits, regulatory backlash, reputational damage
   Mitigation: Explicit opt-in (OFF default), weekly consent refresh, abuse hotline

5. MEDIUM: Gender bias in suggestions (LLM training data)
   → PR crisis, regulatory inquiry
   Mitigation: Quarterly bias audits (A/B test suggestions with diverse cohorts)

6. MEDIUM: AI gives bad health advice (liability)
   → Parent lawsuit if child harmed (e.g., allergy mistake)
   Mitigation: Disclaimers + SaMD assessment + NO health recommendations v1

7. MEDIUM: Data breach / API compromise
   → Loss of trust, regulatory fine, class action
   Mitigation: Encryption in transit/rest, SoC 2 Type II audit

8. LOW-MEDIUM: Children > 13 see parental logs
   → Psychological harm (embarrassment, family tension)
   Mitigation: UX design (parent-only view), don't expose IA analysis to children

9. LOW: Accessibility excludes disabled users
   → DGAC lawsuit (french disability law), legal liability
   Mitigation: WCAG 2.1 AA audit (screen reader, dyslexic fonts)

10. LOW: Regulatory evolution (EU rules change)
    → Retroactive compliance burden
    Mitigation: Subscribe to EDPB + Commission updates, annual DPIA review
