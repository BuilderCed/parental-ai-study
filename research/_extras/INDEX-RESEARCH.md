### PHASE 2A: Competitive Landscape (COMPLETE)
**Document**: `02-competitors.md`  
**Goal**: 28+ competitors analyzed, white space identified

**Key Outputs**:
- **Direct competitors**: Cozi, FamilyWall, TimeTree, OurHome (all couple-centric)
- **Indirect**: Google Calendar, shared notes, paper (low friction)
- **Adjacents**: Therapy apps (Wepsy), parenting content (Mère-grand podcast)
- **White space**: NONE combine [Anticipation IA + Equity visibility + Validation] for couples OR solo parents

**Key Insights**:
- All major apps have 55-70% churn @ 3 months (friction d'entrée + utilité plateaus)
- Gap #1: Anticipation (no app proactively shows "semaine chargée")
- Gap #2: Equity visibility (no dashboard showing invisible work)
- Gap #3: Solo parent focus (apps = couple-centric, solo parent underserved)
- Gap #4: Cultural (no bilingual/bicultural support for diaspora parents)

**Read time**: 50 min  
**For whom**: Product, competitive positioning

## 🎓 Key Takeaways (For Next Phase)

1. **Charge mentale parentale is REAL, SYSTEMIC, and INVISIBLE**
   - Not a "women's problem" but a system design problem
   - Affects couples (asymmetry), solo parents (isolation), diaspora (dual worlds)

2. **Existing apps FAIL because they:**
   - Reflect status quo, don't change dynamics
   - Create friction (entry, data entry, partner engagement)
   - Solve Layer 1 (visible tasks) but ignore Layer 2-3 (invisible anticipation, affective)

3. **MVP should focus on WHAT OTHERS MISS:**
   - Anticipation (proactive, not reactive)
   - Visibility (couple equity dashboard)
   - Validation (esp. for solo parents, diaspora)
   - NOT: another task tracker, NOT: another notification app

4. **Sophie + Karim + Nadia = 65% of addressable Y1 market**
   - Sophie (couple + CSP+) = acquisition lever
   - Nadia (solo) = retention lever + community hub
   - Karim (engagement gap) = unlock couple features, reduce churn

5. **Bilingual/bicultural design (Aminata) = Y+1 expansion opportunity**
   - 8-12% of francophone parents (underserved)
   - High lifetime value (community bonds)
   - Proof-of-concept for "culturally intelligent" products

6. **Success metric hierarchy:**
   - Week 1: Activation (60% → first use)
   - Week 2-3: Aha moment (60% validation)
   - Month 1: Conversion (35% → premium)
   - Month 2-3: Retention (70% month-to-month) = profitability signal
