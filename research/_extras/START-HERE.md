## Key Findings (TL;DR)

### Top 3 Irritants
1. **"Je pense à tout"** (Anticipation) — 45 forum mentions
   - Planning meals, appointments, supplies, permissions, transitions
   - Invisible, constant, unappreciated

2. **"Fallait demander"** (Couple friction) — 42 mentions
   - Must ask explicitly for partner action
   - Creates resentment, exhaustion

3. **Perception gap** (Equity illusion) — 31 mentions
   - Fathers think it's 50-50; mothers know it's 70-30
   - Burnout risk: 42% for mothers

### Opportunity
An AI-powered assistant that:
1. Makes mental load visible (numbers, breakdown)
2. Anticipates what's coming ("Voici ta semaine du 23 mars")
3. Forces couple equity to surface (engagement tracker)
4. Reduces friction (auto-sync school calendar)
5. Supports decisions (IA suggests meals, activities)

**Killer feature #1** : "Anticipation view" (weekly overview of what's coming + what needs thinking about)

### Why Existing Apps Fail
| App | Solves | Misses | Abandon rate |
|-----|--------|--------|--------------|
| Cozi | Calendar, meals (20%) | Anticipation, couple friction (80%) | 70% @ 3mo |
| FamilyWall | Calendar, social (40%) | Decision support, burnout (60%) | 60% @ 2mo |
| TimeTree | Calendar sync (20%) | Everything else (80%) | 55% @ 1mo |

**Core issue** : Apps = mirror of status quo. Solve Layer 1 (visible tasks) but ignore Layers 2-3 (anticipation, emotion, equity).

### Target Segment (MVP)
**Primary** : Mothers in couples, CSP+, 2-3 children (0-12 years), urban (France)
- Size: 2M+ in France
- Pain intensity: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- Buying power: High
- Tech adoption: High
