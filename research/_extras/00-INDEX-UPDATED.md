## 📈 Next Phase: Validation

### Phase 5 Roadmap (Recommended)
1. **Week 1-2** : Niche selection (recommend Parents Solo)
2. **Week 3-4** : MVP design (Anticipation View + Emotional Support Bot)
3. **Week 5-6** : PMI partnership outreach
4. **Week 7-10** : Pilot test (50-100 parents, 4 weeks)
5. **Week 11+** : Scale or pivot based on DAU/NPS/Retention data

### Success Metrics (Set Before Pilot)
- **DAU (Daily Active Users)** : Target > 40% by week 4
- **WAU (Weekly Active Users)** : Target > 60%
- **NPS (Net Promoter Score)** : Target > 40
- **Retention** : Target > 50% at 2 weeks

### Pivot Triggers
- If DAU < 30% by week 4 → immediate pivot to niche 2 or 3
- If NPS < 30 → product redesign or niche pivot
- If CAC > €20 (via organic) → test B2B2C channels (CSE, Mutuelles) instead

## 📞 Questions to Answer Before Launch

1. **Niche Validation**
   - Which niche resonates most with your vision? (Parents Solo vs Santé vs Nombreuses?)
   - Any existing partnerships that could accelerate? (PMI contacts? Mutuelles?)

2. **Go-to-Market**
   - Will you pursue B2C (direct to parents) or B2B2C (via partners) first?
   - Recommended: B2B2C faster to scale (CSE, Mutuelles in parallel)

3. **Compliance**
   - Is mental health content in-scope? (requires higher RGPD compliance)
   - Is medical advice in-scope? (requires liability insurance + disclaimers)

4. **Timeline**
   - Can you build MVP in 2-3 weeks? (Use no-code: Softr, Bubble, or Claude Agent SDK)
   - Can you secure PMI pilot partner by week 5?
